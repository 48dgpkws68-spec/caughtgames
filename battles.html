How a match works

Same game, same seed. The better player wins.

Players are matched one against one under exactly equal conditions. Nobody plays against the house and nothing in the outcome is left to a random number.

FAQ

Questions operators ask.

Is this a game against the house?

No. Players stake against each other. The operator never takes the other side of a match; it earns a fee from the pot of every match that has a winner.

How do you stop collusion and chip dumping?

Stake matches pair players within a capped rating gap, never pair two players from the same network, limit how often the same two players can meet per 24 hours and let players block each other. Your risk team gets a report of repeat pairs, shared networks and early forfeits, plus a full audit record for every match.

Do players ever face a bot?

Not in stake mode. Bots exist only for free practice and are never offered while a player waits for a stake match.

What if the game server restarts mid-match?

If the game server restarts during a stake match, the match does not count and both stakes are refunded automatically. Credits and rollbacks are retried until your wallet confirms them, so money never gets stuck.

Where can real money be played?

Only through licensed operators, in markets where player-versus-player skill games with stakes are allowed. The operator handles KYC, age checks, geo restrictions, limits and responsible gambling tools. Every demo on this site uses play money.

Which settings can we change?

Stakes, currencies and the fee per operator, plus the maximum rating gap, the daily limit per pair, emotes and rematches. Rematches are off by default, because fast rematches can speed up loss chasing.

Can we get a 1v1 game in our own brand?

Talk to us about a branded version of MIRROR or a custom 1v1 title on the same server, wallet flow and replay system.
