Before each heist we publish SHA-256(server seed). The plate order of row r comes from HMAC-SHA256(seed, client:nonce:layout:r), the alarm draw of row r is u = HMAC-SHA256(seed, client:nonce:step:r) / 232. You are caught when u is below the risk of the plate you chose (shadow 10%, dim 30%, spot 55%). After the heist the seed is revealed.

Pay: a safe step multiplies by 1/(1 − risk), the first step by 0.97/(1 − risk). A step that would pass 10,000× pays exactly 10,000× and its risk drops to 1 − multiplier/10,000, so even the max-win run returns 97%.

How to SNEAK

A diamond waits at the end of a guarded hall. Every row has three pressure plates. You choose how loud each step is.

  1. Pick a plate. Shadow trips the alarm 10% of the time, dim 30%, spot 55%. Riskier plates multiply more.
  2. Change your risk every step. Play it safe early, go loud when you feel it, or the other way round.
  3. Cash out any time after the first step. Caught means the stake is gone.
  4. Reach the diamond or hit 10,000× and the thief grabs it automatically.
