Provably fair

Before each tower we publish SHA-256(server seed). Pull n uses u = HMAC-SHA256(seed, client:nonce:pull:n), first 52 bits ÷ 252. The tower falls when u is below the fall chance shown on the block you pulled. After the round the seed is revealed.

Fall chance = base × (0.75 + 0.5 × share of tower above) × (1 + 0.035 × weakened layers + 0.12 × one-block layers). Base: edge from a full layer 5%, centre from a full layer 13%, leaving only the centre 24%, leaving one edge 42 to 46%. A safe pull pays 1 ÷ (1 − fall chance), the first pull 0.97 ÷ (1 − fall chance).

How to play WOBBLE

A rainbow tower, a steady hand and a cash-out button.

  1. Tap a block to see its fall chance and what it pays. Tap again (or PULL) to slide it out.
  2. Edges are steady, centres pay more. Every pull weakens the tower, so the odds change all the time.
  3. Cash out any time after the first pull. If the tower falls, the stake is gone.
  4. Drag or use the arrows to turn the tower and reach every block.
