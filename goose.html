How to play GOOSE 63

The board game everyone played as a kid, now with a cash-out button. Get your goose to square 63.

  1. Pick one die or two. One die moves you a little, two dice move you far. Each button shows the exact chance of landing on a trap.
  2. Geese fly you on. Land on a goose and you move the same number again. The bridge jumps ahead, the maze sends you back.
  3. Avoid the traps. The inn (19), the well (31), the prison (52) and the skull (58) end the game.
  4. Cash out any time after your first risky throw, or land exactly on 63 for the golden goose.
