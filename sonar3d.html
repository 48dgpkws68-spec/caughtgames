Before each dive we publish SHA-256(server seed). Mine positions are a Fisher-Yates shuffle over the 36 clams driven by HMAC-SHA256(seed, client:nonce:i), first 32 bits ÷ 232. Ping k is honest when HMAC-SHA256(seed, client:nonce:ping:k) is below 0.8, otherwise it reads the other way. After the dive the seed is revealed so anyone can rebuild the reef.

How to play SONAR 3D

Sea mines hide in a 6×6 bed of clams. Your little sub dives where you tap, and the sonar tells you exactly how dangerous the water around it is. Play money only.

  1. Dive a clam. The first one is blind and places your stake.
  2. Read the number. It counts the mines in the 8 clams touching it. A clam with no number has no mines around it, so its neighbours open for free. Clams the numbers prove are mines get a red flag.
  3. Risk pays, safety is free. Each clam pays its true odds minus a risk fee. A clam proven safe pays ×1.00 but gives you more sonar.
  4. Ping when stuck. 10% of your stake reads one clam warm or cold, right 80% of the time. Up to 3 per dive.
  5. Cash out any time after your first clam. Hit a mine and the stake is gone.
