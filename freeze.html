Provably fair

Before each round we publish SHA-256(server seed). The crash point is c = floor(100 × 0.97 ÷ (1 − u)) ÷ 100 with u = HMAC-SHA256(seed, client:nonce:round), first 52 bits ÷ 252, at least 1.00 and at most 10,000. So P(c ≥ x) = 0.97 ÷ x and every freeze moment returns 97%. The owl's half-turns are decoration and never end a round. After the round the seed is revealed.

How to play FREEZE

Grandmother's footsteps, with the whole room on one field and a very large owl.

  1. Join a round. Put in your stake while the countdown runs. Your runner wears the gold crown.
  2. Everybody runs. The multiplier climbs while the pack creeps toward the owl.
  3. Freeze to win. Tap FREEZE and your runner turns into an ice statue with the multiplier locked in.
  4. Don't get caught. When the owl turns round, every runner still moving loses. Half-turns are just the owl teasing.
