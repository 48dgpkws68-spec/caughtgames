Services we use

  • Umami, open-source visitor statistics that we host ourselves on play.caughtgames.com. It sets no cookies and does not store your IP address.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers the demo request form to our mailbox.
  • Microsoft 365 hosts our email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like any web server it processes your IP address to deliver the pages.
  • Google Fonts, cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDelivr deliver fonts and the 3D code libraries for the demos. Your browser connects to them directly, so they see your IP address.

How long we keep it

Form requests and emails are kept as long as we are in contact about a possible deal or work together, and are deleted when you ask. Visitor statistics contain no names, email addresses or IP addresses.
