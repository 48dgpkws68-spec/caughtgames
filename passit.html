Before each round we publish SHA-256(server seed). Every number is u = HMAC-SHA256(seed, client:nonce:message), first 52 bits ÷ 252.

Crash. c = floor(100 × 0.97 ÷ (1 − uround)) ÷ 100, at least 1.00 and at most 10,000, so P(c ≥ x) = 0.97 ÷ x. A cash-out at ×m wins when m ≤ c (cash-outs start at ×1.01). If the multiplier reaches ×10,000 the fuse burns out and everyone still in is paid ×10,000.

Bomb route. Pass k uses upass:k and never looks at c. The next holder is any seat except the current one, weight 4 for your seat when you play this round and 1 for every other seat. x = u × total weight picks the seat, and the leftover f inside that seat's weight (uniform 0 to 1) sets the hold: 1.5 + f s in your hands, 0.8 + 0.7 f s for others, plus a 0.48 s throw. Because the route ignores c, any rule for when you cash returns 97%.

How to play PASS IT

Hot potato at a party table, with a real fuse and the whole room playing one round.

  1. Take a seat. Put in your stake while the countdown runs. You sit at the front with the gold crown.
  2. Pass it. The bomb hops from hand to hand every second or two while the multiplier climbs for everyone.
  3. Cash out while you hold it. When the bomb lands in your hands, the CASH OUT button lights up. Take the multiplier or let it fly on.
  4. Boom. When the bomb goes off, everyone who has not cashed out loses. It does not matter who holds it.
