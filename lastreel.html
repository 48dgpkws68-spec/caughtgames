Provably fair

Before each spin we publish SHA-256(server seed). Reel i (1 to 5) uses ui = HMAC-SHA256(seed, client:nonce:reel:i), first 52 bits ÷ 252, and stops at floor(ui × strip length). That stop is the middle row; the rows above and below follow the strip. Free spin n uses the messages fs:n:reel:1 to fs:n:reel:5 on the free spin strips, with the same seed and nonce. Reel 5's stop is fixed by the seed before you choose to double, so the choice cannot change where it lands.

Double: k = 0.965 ÷ E, where E is the average over all reel 5 stops of what reel 5 adds to the line wins (right to left wins plus the step up to 5 of a kind), in multiples of the stake. The extra stake pays k × that amount; tickets and free spins are paid by the spin stake only. The seed is revealed when the spin, and any free spins it started, are over.

How to play LAST REEL

Five candy reels, ten lines, one big decision and a golden bonus.

  1. Press SPIN. Your stake covers 10 lines. Lines pay left to right and right to left, 3 or more in a row. The star is wild.
  2. Reels 1 to 4 land, reel 5 keeps turning. The cabinet lights every win it can still complete. DOUBLE adds one extra stake that pays k × whatever reel 5 adds. Either way the game returns 96.5%.
  3. 3 golden tickets anywhere start 10 FREE SPINS. Every free spin raises the multiplier: ×1, ×2, ×3 and on up to ×20. 3 tickets again add 5 more spins.
  4. Wins go up to 10,000× your stake. Play money only.

Paytable LAST REEL

Amounts for a stake of 10.00 (line bet 1.00).

Free spins

  • 3, 4 or 5 golden tickets anywhere on screen pay 1×, 10× or 100× the stake and start 10 FREE SPINS. A ticket landing on reel 5 counts like any other.
  • Free spin n pays every win × n (×1, ×2, ×3 and on, up to ×20). The free spin reels carry stacked wilds and more of the big symbols.
  • 3 or more tickets during free spins add 5 more spins (about 1 in 87 free spins) and the multiplier keeps climbing.
  • Free spins start about 1 in 161 spins (exact: 265,410 of 42,765,030 stop combinations) and pay 55.8× the stake on average. 37% of them pay 50× or more, 12% pay 100× or more and about 1 in 2,850 pays 1,000× or more.

10 lines, both ways

  • Wins count from reel 1 to the right and from reel 5 to the left, 3 or more adjacent symbols on a line. A 5 of a kind pays once. Wins on different lines add up.
  • The wild star stands in for every symbol except the ticket and also pays on its own.
  • Double is offered when reel 5 can still add a line win. The extra stake pays k × what reel 5 adds to the line wins (right to left wins and the step up to 5 of a kind). k = 0.965 ÷ the exact average of that amount over the reel 5 stops, so every double returns 96.5%. Tickets and free spins are paid by the spin stake, never by the double.
  • RTP 96.50% (96.504%): line wins 60.93% (exact, all 42,765,030 stop combinations), tickets 0.94%, free spins 34.63% (exact recursion over the free spin strips). Max win 10,000× stake for a spin and its free spins: a single free spin screen can pay up to 1,287× before the multiplier, so the cap can be reached from ×8 on. A double is settled on its own stake (at most about 34× it).
