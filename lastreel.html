Provably fair

Before each spin we publish SHA-256(server seed). Reel i (1 to 5) uses ui = HMAC-SHA256(seed, client:nonce:reel:i), first 52 bits ÷ 252, and stops at floor(ui × strip length). That stop is the middle row; the rows above and below follow the strip. Reel 5's stop is fixed by the seed before you choose to double, so the choice cannot change where it lands.

Double: k = 0.965 ÷ E, where E is the average over all reel 5 stops of what reel 5 adds to the win (right to left wins plus the step up to 5 of a kind), in multiples of the stake. The extra stake pays k × that amount. After the spin the seed is revealed.

How to play LAST REEL

Five candy reels, ten lines and one big decision.

  1. Press SPIN. Your stake covers 10 lines. Lines pay left to right and right to left, 3 or more in a row. The star is wild.
  2. Reels 1 to 4 land, reel 5 keeps turning. The cabinet lights every win it can still complete and shows how many of its stops hit.
  3. DOUBLE adds one extra stake that only plays on reel 5. It pays k × whatever reel 5 adds. A long shot gets a big k, a likely hit a small one.
  4. Or let it land. Either way the game returns 96.5%. Play money only.

Paytable LAST REEL

Amounts for a stake of 10.00 (line bet 1.00).

10 lines, both ways

  • Wins count from reel 1 to the right and from reel 5 to the left, 3 or more adjacent symbols on a line. A 5 of a kind pays once. Wins on different lines add up.
  • The wild star stands in for every symbol and also pays on its own.
  • Double is offered when reel 5 can still add a win. The extra stake pays k × what reel 5 adds (right to left wins and the step up to 5 of a kind). k = 0.965 ÷ the exact average of that amount over the reel 5 stops.
  • Base game RTP 96.4995% (exact, all 36,765,696 stop combinations). Each double returns 96.5%. Max win 10,000× stake per spin; the highest possible screen pays 290.8× and a doubled spin at most 299.5×.
