How to play SQUISH JAR

A glass jar, twelve squishy blobs and real physics. Play money only.

  1. Set your stake and press SQUISH. Twelve blobs of random sizes drop one by one into an empty jar.
  2. Two equal blobs that touch melt into the next size up and pay the table. If the new blob melts again within half a second, that is a chain: link 2 pays ×2, link 3 pays ×3 and so on.
  3. Grow a golden blob for the jackpot of .
  4. Nothing to decide but the stake. The fair draws pick the blobs, the physics does the rest. Space spins, T turbo, A auto.
