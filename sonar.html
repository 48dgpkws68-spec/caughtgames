How to read SONAR

Mines hide under a 6×6 patch of sea. You decide where to dive, and the sonar tells you exactly how dangerous the water around you is.

  1. Dive a tile. The first tile is blind. It places your stake.
  2. Read the number. It counts the mines among the 8 tiles touching it. A 0 means all neighbours are safe.
  3. Risk pays, safety is free. Each tile pays its true odds minus a risk fee. A tile the numbers prove safe pays ×1.00 but gives you more sonar.
  4. Ping when stuck. 10% of your stake buys a warm/cold reading on one tile, right 80% of the time. Up to 3 per dive.
  5. Cash out any time. After the dive, the debrief shows the safest tile at every move.
